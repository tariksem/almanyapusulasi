The basics

Read the Hausordnung when moving in. It may cover entrance doors, stairwell cleaning, shared laundry rooms and waste disposal.

How to proceed

Quiet hours and restrictions on noisy work, particularly on Sundays and public holidays, depend on local law and house rules. Check before drilling or renovating.

Important details

A brief greeting is a good way to meet neighbours. When noise becomes a problem, try a polite conversation first; keep notes and contact the Hausverwaltung if it continues.

Mistakes and practical advice

Do not assume storage in corridors, balcony barbecues or leaving shoes in shared areas is permitted. Fire escape routes must remain clear.
