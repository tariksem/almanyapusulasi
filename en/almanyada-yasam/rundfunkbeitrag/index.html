Rundfunkbeitrag: the household broadcasting contribution

What households, flatmates and eligible exemptions need to know.

The basics

In Germany the public broadcasting contribution is generally assessed per dwelling, rather than separately for every resident.

How to proceed

If someone else in the flat already pays, you may need to notify the contribution service and provide the payer's Beitragsnummer.

Important details

Exemptions and reductions are not automatic for everyone. Certain social benefits or disability-related circumstances may qualify under specific rules; verify conditions on the official website.

Mistakes and practical advice

Do not ignore contribution letters. Check which dwelling and time period they concern, and use rundfunkbeitrag.de for official procedures.

Everyday practical cases

Broadcasting contribution: three typical household situations

  1. If you have moved into a home alone, check whether the dwelling is registered and use the official Beitragsservice to notify your details. Not watching public broadcasting does not automatically remove the obligation.

  2. In a shared flat (WG), normally one contribution is due for the dwelling. If a housemate already pays, you may need to give their nine-digit Beitragsnummer when responding to the service.

  3. When the person paying moves out, review the registration: the Beitragsnummer belongs to the person and generally moves with them, not with the flat.

  4. Exemptions and reductions are not automatic for everyone with low income. Check the official Befreiung/Ermäßigung conditions and submit the required documents where eligible.

  5. When a letter arrives, verify the dwelling, contribution period, Aktenzeichen and reference number. Avoid lookalike websites; use rundfunkbeitrag.de.

Frequently asked questions

Must I pay without a TV?

The general dwelling-based obligation does not depend on owning a television or using the broadcasters.

Does everyone in a WG pay separately?

Normally no, just one contribution per dwelling. Report the existing payer's reference when requested.

Does one exempt flatmate exempt everyone?

Not generally. The conditions for exemptions in shared dwellings must be assessed carefully.
