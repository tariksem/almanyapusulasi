Finding doctors and getting urgent help

Understanding Hausarzt, specialists, 116117 and 112.

The basics

A Hausarzt is often the first contact for everyday health concerns. Call the practice for an appointment or use its online booking services.

How to proceed

For urgent but non-life-threatening conditions outside regular consultation hours, 116117 provides medical guidance and access to out-of-hours care. Language availability may vary.

Important details

Call 112 for life-threatening emergencies, such as loss of consciousness, severe breathing difficulty or serious injuries. Do not wait for an ordinary appointment.

Mistakes and practical advice

Bring your health insurance card, medication list and relevant medical documents. Ask a specialist practice whether a referral is required.

Practical examples and steps

When should you contact a GP, 116117 or 112?

  1. Everyday medical concerns,Contact a Hausarzt practice for non-emergency symptoms, prescriptions and follow-up care. If you have no GP, ask your insurer or regional physician directory for help finding a practice.

  2. Urgent but not life-threatening outside office hours,If an illness on a weekend cannot reasonably wait until the next consultation, 116117 connects you to out-of-hours medical support. It is not a universal substitute for hospital emergency services.

  3. Life-threatening emergency,For loss of consciousness, severe breathing problems or major injuries, dial 112 immediately. Do not delay urgent medical help in a serious situation.

  4. Prepare for the appointment,Take your insurance card, medication list, allergies and relevant medical reports. Write down when symptoms began to explain them more clearly.

  5. A useful German phrase,You can say 'Ich brauche einen Termin' (I need an appointment) or 'Ich habe seit gestern Schmerzen' (I have been in pain since yesterday). Plan language help if needed.

Frequently asked questions

How are 116117 and 112 different?

116117 is for urgent non-life-threatening medical needs outside regular hours; 112 is for life-threatening emergencies.

Do I always need a referral for a specialist?

Requirements depend on the practice and service. Ask whether an Überweisung is needed.

Can I expect service in English?

Language support is not guaranteed in all situations. Consider arranging assistance when possible.
