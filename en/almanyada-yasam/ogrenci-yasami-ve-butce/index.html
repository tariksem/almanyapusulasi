The basics

After admission, check your Semesterbeitrag, student ID, transport entitlements and re-enrolment dates. Semesterticket coverage is not identical at every institution.

How to proceed

For WG shared flats, ask what rent includes, how the deposit works and what rules apply to shared spaces. Verify the room and agreement before making payments.

Important details

University Mensa canteens, libraries and Studierendenwerk services can help with housing, finance and counselling. Find your local student services organisation.

Mistakes and practical advice

Budget separately for rent, insurance, phone, food, study materials and transport. BAföG funding and scholarships depend on personal circumstances and eligibility.

Practical examples and steps

A student budget and first-month checklist

  1. Split your budget into categories,Illustration only: €500 for a WG room, €180 for food, €130 for insurance and €40 for phone/other costs, totalling €850 per month. Actual costs vary greatly by location and insurance status.

  2. Check semester contributions and transport,Confirm the Semesterbeitrag, student card and Semesterticket/Deutschlandsemesterticket coverage with the university. Rules differ between institutions.

  3. Review your WG tenancy,Check total rent, shared service charges, deposit and who signs the lease. Do not transfer money before verifying the property and landlord.

  4. Use Studierendenwerk advice,Look for housing, social and financial counselling and possible psychosocial support with your local student services organisation.

  5. Organise documents and deadlines,Keep enrolment confirmation, health insurance documents, Anmeldung and semester renewal dates. BAföG and scholarships have separate eligibility conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Is every international student eligible for BAföG?

No. Immigration status, course, income and other requirements matter.

Does a semester ticket cover all of Germany?

Coverage depends on the university's current ticket type and applicable rules.

Is Studierendenwerk only for dormitories?

No. Many organisations also provide social and other counselling.
