Registering your address: Anmeldung

How to prepare for your Bürgeramt appointment after moving.

Reviewed: 25 November 2026

The basics

As a general rule, you must register at your new address within two weeks of moving into a home. Exceptions exist in particular circumstances, so check local guidance.

How to proceed

Book an appointment with your Bürgeramt or Meldebehörde. You will commonly need identity documents and a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung completed by your housing provider.

Important details

Anmeldung is not a residence permit. Address registration, tax identification numbers, residence cards and banking involve different processes.

Mistakes and practical advice

Check that the name, move-in date and address match across your documents. Keep the Meldebestätigung certificate issued by the registration office.

Practical steps

Anmeldung: four steps after moving

  1. Record the actual move-in date. Under section 17 BMG, the general registration deadline is two weeks after occupation, not contract signing.

  2. Get a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung from the authorised housing provider. The tenancy agreement does not invariably replace it.

  3. Check the official Bürgeramt/Meldebehörde booking system. If online registration is supported locally, check identity requirements.

  4. Keep the Meldebestätigung and update your address separately with bank, insurer, employer, school and immigration authority when relevant.

Frequently asked questions

What if appointments are unavailable?

Keep evidence of booking attempts and ask the Meldebehörde how to proceed; do not assume an automatic exception.

Is online registration universal?

No. Availability and identification requirements vary.
