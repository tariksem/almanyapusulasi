The basics

Friendships often grow through repeated contact. Attend the same sports Verein, course or volunteering group over several weeks instead of trying many one-off events.

How to proceed

Look for affordable activities at a Stadtteiltreff, Volkshochschule, public library or your city's Veranstaltungskalender.

Important details

If your German is limited, international groups can be a first step, but local activities help with language immersion. Suggest a coffee or another small shared activity after a few meetings.

Mistakes and practical advice

People may take time to build close friendships. A slow start does not necessarily mean rejection. Choose one regular weekly activity and stick with it for a while.
