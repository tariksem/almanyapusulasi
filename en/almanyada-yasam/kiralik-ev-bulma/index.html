The basics

Separate Kaltmiete, Nebenkosten and Heizkosten when reading listings; together they determine the housing budget. Ask about the deposit, kitchen, energy costs and move-in date.

How to proceed

Prepare a short rental application with an introduction, proof of income and a SCHUFA report if appropriate. Do not assume that every document requested is always necessary or lawful.

Important details

Do not pay before verifying the flat, the other party and the contract. Be cautious when someone claims to be abroad and offers to mail the keys after an advance payment.

Mistakes and practical advice

At handover, photograph the property, complete an Übergabeprotokoll and record meter readings. Request a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung and understand the deposit terms.
