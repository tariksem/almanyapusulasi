Phone, internet and subscription contracts

Contract duration, renewals, cancellation and moving home.

The basics

Before agreeing, check the monthly price, installation charges, minimum term, promised speed and whether the price increases after an introductory discount.

How to proceed

For phone and internet services, obtain and keep the Vertragszusammenfassung. Compare written terms with any sales promises.

Important details

Moving does not automatically terminate every contract. Whether service can be supplied at the new address and whether special cancellation rights apply depends on the contract.

Mistakes and practical advice

Keep evidence of cancellation requests and check the Kündigungsfrist. Coordinate the start and end dates of providers to avoid paying twice.

Practical examples and steps

Three real-life phone and internet contract scenarios

  1. Calculate the full promotional price,For illustration: six months at €20 and 18 months at €40 total €840 over 24 months, averaging €35 per month before setup or device charges.

  2. Check telephone offers in writing,Read the Vertragszusammenfassung for price, speed, duration and cancellation. If the summary is supplied after a telephone sale, additional approval in text form may be necessary for a valid agreement.

  3. Moving home,If the same service can be supplied at your new address, the existing contract generally continues. If unavailable there, a special termination right with one month's notice may apply under the relevant conditions.

  4. Slow service or outages,Use the appropriate official Breitbandmessung documentation process. Statutory reduction or cancellation remedies may depend on specific evidence; one speed test alone is not enough.

  5. Keep cancellation evidence,Store the confirmation, termination date and the new provider's activation date. Written records help resolve disputes.

Frequently asked questions

Does moving automatically cancel internet?

No. Availability at the new address and legal conditions matter.

Is one speed test sufficient?

Usually not. Documentation procedures apply.

Can I compare just the introductory price?

No. Add charges over the full minimum term, including setup and equipment.
