Daycare and school in Germany

Childcare applications, parent communication and after-school options.

Reviewed: 25 November 2026

The basics

Kita applications and deadlines depend on your municipality. Look up the local Kita portal or Jugendamt well before you need a place.

How to proceed

School enrolment procedures and school types vary by federal state. Keep track of letters, parent apps, appointments with teachers and Elternabend invitations.

Important details

Lunches, Ganztag programmes, after-school care and excursions may involve separate registration or charges. Ask about support for eligible families.

Mistakes and practical advice

Bring clear questions to teacher meetings. Raise learning, language or additional support needs early so the school can explain available options.

Practical guide: Real-world steps

A practical family application timeline

  1. Children aged one and over generally have an entitlement to daycare or child day care. This does not guarantee a place in a particular Kita; check the local application portal early.

  2. Find the municipal deadlines, care hours and supporting documents. The Jugendamt can explain the local application system.

  3. The phased all-day primary school entitlement began with first-grade children on 1 August 2026, with extension to grades one through four planned by school year 2029/30. Check after-school OGS/Hort enrolment separately from school registration.

  4. In the first school week clarify parent meetings, teacher contacts, lunches, sickness reporting and collection permissions.

Frequently asked questions

Does the entitlement guarantee my preferred Kita?

No. The general entitlement is not an unconditional place at a particular provider.

Must I apply for all-day care separately?

Processes vary locally; check school and municipality announcements.
