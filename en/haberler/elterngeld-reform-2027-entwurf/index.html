Parental allowance 2027: €330–€1,900 proposal, not law yet

The 30-second summary

Families planning a baby may see changes to Elterngeld, Germany's parental allowance. Proposed new payment limits do not apply yet.

Government bill · Not in force

What is changing?

The proposed minimum Basiselterngeld would rise from €300 to €330 and the maximum from €1,800 to €1,900. The total 14 months would remain, with four months reserved for each parent and six months to share. Single parents would retain up to 14 months.

Who is affected?

Expectant parents and people planning a family, especially households wishing to divide childcare between both parents.

Simple example

A child born today does not automatically qualify for the proposed €330 minimum. The new law must first be enacted.

What should you do?

  • Use the current Elterngeld rules for applications being made now.
  • When planning leave, remember that the proposed changes are not yet binding.
  • Wait for the enacted law and transitional rules based on your child's birth date.
Important: Legal status

The government aims for 1 November 2027. Both the planned start and the amounts may change during legislation.
