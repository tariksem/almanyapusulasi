German social insurance ceilings: What changes in 2027?

The 30-second summary

High earners may see a change in how their social insurance contributions are calculated in 2027. It does not mean everyone will pay the same additional amount.

Bundesrat approved: 20 November 2026 · Scheduled from 1 January 2027

What is changing?

The approved 2027 monthly ceiling for health and long-term care contributions is €6,375 (2026: €5,812.50). The annual threshold for compulsory statutory health insurance is €84,150 (€7,012.50 monthly). The general pension contribution ceiling is €8,850 monthly.

Who is affected?

Especially employees with higher gross salaries and those considering statutory versus private health insurance. Workers below the affected ceilings are not all affected in the same way.

Simple example

Someone earning €7,000 gross per month may see a different health-insurance contribution if the ceiling changes. The exact net impact depends on individual insurance and contribution rates.

What should you do?

  • Bundesrat approval is complete; check official promulgation before the planned 1 January 2027 start.
  • Review the contribution base and insurance rates on your first 2027 payslip.
  • Do not choose private insurance on the basis of a single salary threshold; consider long-term costs.
Important: Legal status

The Bundesrat approved the ordinance on 20 November 2026. The new thresholds are scheduled to apply from 1 January 2027. Publication in the Federal Law Gazette should be checked separately; 2026 thresholds continue to apply during 2026.
