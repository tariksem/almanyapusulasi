Deutschlandticket will cost €66.80 per month in 2027

The 30-second summary

The monthly Germany-wide local transport pass will cost €66.80 instead of €63 from 1 January 2027.

Announced tariff · From 1 January 2027

What is changing?

The new monthly fare is €66.80. Many subscriptions will update automatically, but some providers need the subscriber to approve the change and will contact customers.

Who is affected?

Deutschlandticket subscribers, people commuting to work or study, and employees using an employer-supported Jobticket.

Simple example

If you currently pay €63, the new monthly cost is €3.80 higher. Over twelve months that is €45.60 more.

What should you do?

  • Check your ticket app and email for the new fare notice.
  • If your provider requests approval, do not miss its deadline.
  • If you no longer need the pass, review your subscription's cancellation conditions.
Important: Legal status

The ticket generally does not cover long-distance ICE, IC or EC services. Local discounts and Jobticket terms may vary.
