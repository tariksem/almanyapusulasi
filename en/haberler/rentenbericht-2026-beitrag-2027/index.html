German pension contributions: 18.6% in 2027

The 30-second summary

Germany's annual pension report indicates that the statutory pension insurance contribution rate will remain 18.6% in 2027.

Official report · Model calculations

What is changing?

The government report states that the contribution rate remains at 18.6% in 2027. It also models a total increase of around 46.2% in pensions by 2040. This is a projection based on assumptions, not a guaranteed personal increase.

Who is affected?

Employees paying into the German pension system, people planning retirement and current pensioners.

Simple example

If you receive a pension today, do not assume that the percentage in the report is a guaranteed increase to your own monthly payment.

What should you do?

  • Check the contribution rate and earnings ceiling on your 2027 payslip.
  • Consult your personal Renteninformation from Deutsche Rentenversicherung.
  • Treat long-term model results as scenarios, not a firm promise of future payments.
Important: Legal status

The 2026 government pension report uses model calculations based on assumptions and existing or proposed measures. It does not fix individual pensions.
